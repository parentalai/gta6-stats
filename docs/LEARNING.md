# Learning notes

Short explanations of concepts as they first appear in the project (2-3 sentences each).

## Trophy / achievement rarity
PlayStation and Xbox record, for every trophy or achievement, the percentage of a game's players who have earned it. Watching those percentages over time shows how fast the player base moves through the game, and multiplying by an estimated player count turns them into absolute numbers ("~N people fixed Mandy's sink"). That multiplication is an estimate, so the site always shows the formula.
